# Staff Guide — Editing the Public Website

For Moreh Academy staff who use the admin portal. No technical knowledge needed.

Sign in to the portal with your staff account, then use the menu on the left.

Everything in this guide lives under **System Administration**. Only the main administrator sees those screens — if a page isn't in your menu, your account doesn't have that permission. That's normal, not a fault.

---

## 0. Which screen changes what

| Screen | Changes | Public pages affected |
|---|---|---|
| **Homepage Hero** | The big banner: headline, tagline, description, two buttons, three slide photos | Homepage |
| **Homepage Sections** | The five values, the About block and its photo, the Latest Updates card, the footer contact lines | Homepage, plus the footer on every interior page |
| **Page Banners** | The navy title banner and the heading block under it, page by page | every interior page |
| **Website Pages** | Longer page text and the photo galleries | every interior page |
| **About Moreh Academy** (Institutional) | Vision, mission, core values, leadership team and history timeline | About Us pages |

All four screens show a preview and a **Save** button. Nothing you type is public until you save. Cleared boxes fall back to the approved standard wording, so you can't leave the site blank.

---

## 1. The homepage banner

**System Administration → Homepage Hero**

The banner is the block at the top of the homepage: headline, tagline, short description, two buttons, and three photos that slide behind them.

1. A preview of the banner sits at the top of the screen. It looks like the real homepage.
2. Type in any box below — the preview changes as you type.
3. Press **Save & publish**. The website updates straight away and you get a green confirmation.
4. **View homepage** opens the live page. **Reload saved version** throws away typing you haven't saved.

| Box | What it does | Keep it to |
|---|---|---|
| Main headline | The biggest text in the banner | about 60 characters |
| Tagline | The italic line under the headline | one short line |
| Description | The small sentence under that | one or two sentences |
| Primary button label | Wording of the orange button | 3–4 words |
| Primary button link | Where it goes | see below |
| Secondary button label | Wording of the outlined button | 3–5 words |
| Secondary button link | Where it goes | see below |

### Links for the buttons

- **Another website:** a full address starting with `https://`
- **A page on our own site:** the page name from the top menu, for example `?page=admissions`, `?page=about`, or `?page=create-account` for the enrolment form

A link that doesn't start with `https://` or `?page=` won't save; if you see an error mentioning the link, that's why.

### The three slide photos

Use **Upload photo** under each photo box (see section 5). Leave a box empty to keep that slide's default picture.

**Tip:** the first slide is the one most visitors see. Use your strongest landscape photo there and keep the headline short — on a phone it sits across the middle of the picture.

## 2. The rest of the homepage

**System Administration → Homepage Sections**

The preview at the top shows the sections as they appear under the banner. Press **Save & publish** when they look right.

### Values strip

The five short labels in the blue band under the banner — Quality Education, Holistic Development, and so on. The icons stay the same; only the wording changes. Keep each one short (about three words).

### About section

- **Small line above the heading** — the tiny orange-label text
- **Heading** — the large line, e.g. *Building a Strong Foundation for Lifelong Success*
- **Paragraph** — the two or three sentences beside the photo
- **Photo** — the picture to the left of the text; use **Upload photo**
- **Photo description** — read aloud by screen readers to people who can't see the picture; describe it in a few words

The **Learn More** button under this paragraph always points at the About page.

### Latest Updates card

This card normally fills itself from your events and announcements. You can take over its contents:

- **Card heading** — the title in the dark blue card
- **Card lines 1–4** — each has a month badge (NOV), a day badge (18), a title and a small line for the venue or a note

Fill in a title to show your own line. Blank rows are ignored. **Leave every row blank** and the card goes back to pulling live announcements and upcoming events. The dates you type are only labels — they don't create real events.

### Footer contact lines

Location, phone and email at the bottom of the page. These appear in the footer of the homepage **and every interior page**, so this is the one place to change the school's contact details.

- Keep the small icons at the start if you want them to stay.
- On the interior pages the email line becomes a clickable mail link when it holds a real address; on the homepage it shows as plain text. Either way, if the line isn't an address it stays ordinary text rather than a broken link.
- Clearing a box restores the standard wording.

## 3. Page banners and headings

**System Administration → Page Banners**

Every interior page has two editable layers:

- **The banner** — the navy strip with a small line, a big title and a subtitle
- **The heading block** — the small line, the heading and the short paragraph at the top of the page body

1. Pick the page from the **Page** dropdown (grouped by section — About Us, Admissions, Academics, and so on). All 31 interior pages are there.
2. The preview shows the banner and the heading block together, exactly as they stack on the page.
3. Edit the boxes. **Save banner** saves the navy strip; **Save intro text** saves the heading block. They're separate so you can change one without touching the other.
4. **Reset to standard** and **Reset intro** remove your changes for that page only.
5. **View this page** opens that page on the live site.

| Box | Keep it to |
|---|---|
| Banner — small line above the title | about 30 characters |
| Banner — page title | about 50 characters |
| Banner — subtitle | one short sentence |
| Banner — background photo | use **Upload photo**, or leave blank for the navy banner |
| Heading — small line above the heading | about 30 characters |
| Heading — page heading | about 50 characters |
| Heading — paragraph under the heading | one or two sentences |

A dark overlay is added automatically to a banner photo so the white text stays readable.

**Two pages have no paragraph under their heading** (Vision, Mission & Core Values, and Frequently Asked Questions). Those show a note instead of a paragraph box — there's nothing to edit.

**Pages edited in Website Pages show a note, not boxes.** Once a page's content has been written on the Website Pages screen, its heading and paragraph belong to that content, so the Page Banners screen steps aside. To control the heading from Page Banners again, clear that page's text in Website Pages.

## 4. Website Pages — longer text and photo galleries

**System Administration → Website Pages**

The list on the left holds eight groups: Home page, About Us, Admissions, Academics, Campus Life, Downloadable Forms, News & Updates and Contact Information. Each group opens every section below it — an introduction, plus the sub-pages (Kindergarten, Requirements, Scholarships and so on).

### Editing text

1. Type in the box for a section.
2. Press **Preview** to check how it will look.
3. Press **Save text**.

Simple formatting works:

- A blank line starts a new paragraph — if you type plain sentences with no tags at all, each line becomes its own paragraph
- `<strong>bold words</strong>` for bold
- `<ul><li>one</li><li>two</li></ul>` for a bullet list

Links can't be added inside these boxes — use the homepage banner buttons for those.

A box that says *Nothing saved yet* means the page is still showing its standard text; type over it to replace it, and the page keeps your version from then on.

### Managing photos and captions

Each section with photos has a **Photos** area:

- **Upload photo** — choose a picture from your phone or computer. It's shrunk automatically, so you don't need to resize anything first, and the button shows *Uploading…* until it's done. The photo joins the gallery straight away, with no separate save step.
- **Caption** — type under any photo and press **Save caption**
- **Remove** — deletes that photo from the page (asks first)

The count next to **Upload photo** tells you how many are there. **The first photo is also used as that page's picture**, so put the best one first.

Photo limits: most interior pages hold **one** photo each; **History** and **Campus Facilities & Tour** hold up to **twelve**.

If photos were added before this version of the portal, they still show. The first time you add, caption or remove one, the whole list moves over to the current page name by itself.

## 5. Uploading photos

Five screens have **Upload photo** buttons. They all shrink the picture in your browser first, so you never need to resize anything by hand — but they don't all behave the same way, so here is the whole picture.

| Where | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Homepage Hero** — the three slide boxes | fills that photo box; the banner uses it after you press **Save & publish** |
| **Homepage Sections** — About section | fills the photo box; shown after you press **Save & publish** |
| **Page Banners** — background photo | fills the box; shown after you press **Save banner** |
| **Website Pages** — each gallery | adds the photo to that page's gallery **straight away** |
| **About Moreh Academy** — each leadership card | shrinks the portrait and stores it with that leader; kept when you press **Save changes** |

### Photos that go into a box

The homepage slides, the About photo and the page banner photo.

1. Press **Upload photo** and choose the file. The button reads *Uploading…* while it works.
2. The box fills with a Google Drive link — that link is what the page uses.
3. Press **Save** on that screen. The photo is **not** public until you do.

These photos are made visible to anyone with the link so the public page can display them. If an upload stops with a message saying Drive would not share the photo, the school photos folder needs to be shared as *Anyone with the link* — ask the administrator to fix that once, then try again.

### Gallery photos on Website Pages

1. Press **Upload photo** in the Photos area of the section you're editing.
2. Choose the file. It's added to that page's gallery **immediately** — there's no extra save step, and the photo strip refreshes with the new picture at the end.
3. Type a caption under it if you want one, then press **Save caption**.

Gallery photos land in the same school photos folder, but the portal delivers them itself, so the sharing message above doesn't apply to them.

### Leadership photos on the About page

The **About Moreh Academy** page (under Institutional) has an **Upload photo** button on every person in the Key Leadership Team.

1. Press **Upload photo** on that person's card and choose the file.
2. It's shrunk to a small portrait — good for a head-and-shoulders photo, not a full-body shot.
3. Press **Save changes** at the top of that screen. Until you do, the photo is only in the editing session.

The picture shown beside that person on the public About page is this same photo, so a clear, well-lit head-and-shoulders shot works best.

### The same in every case

- Big phone photos are fine — they're scaled down automatically. If a picture is still too large, you'll be told to choose a smaller one.
- Landscape photos work best for banners and slides; gallery photos are cropped to fill their frame.
- Where there's a link box, you can still paste a Google Drive thumbnail link instead, in the form `https://drive.google.com/thumbnail?id=FILE_ID&sz=w2000`.

**Not covered here:** two other screens upload pictures with their own buttons — **ID Artwork** (card backgrounds and logos, labelled just *Upload*) and the student photo on a student record. Those are separate jobs with their own screens; ask the administrator if you need them.

## 6. Using the College roster

**College → College Roster**

A live, read-only list of college students from the College SIS workbook. You can look things up; you can't change student records here. Corrections go in the College SIS workbook.

### The four numbers at the top

| Card | Means |
|---|---|
| College students | Students matching what you've filtered, and how many exist on record overall |
| Programs | How many active programs, plus the largest one |
| Year levels | How many year levels and semesters are on record |
| Male / Female | Split from the Gender column |

### Finding a student

1. **Search box:** type a name, student ID, or registration number. The table filters as you type.
2. **Dropdowns:** narrow by program, year level, or semester.
3. **Clear** resets everything.

The bottom of the table says *Showing 120 of 480*, for example. One screen holds up to 400 students; if the second number is bigger, there are more matches than can be displayed — add a filter or search for the student by name to narrow it down.

### Reading a row

Student ID (with the registration number beneath), name (with contact number), program, year level, section (with room), semester (with school year), and status.

### Managing programs

Press **Manage Programs** at the top right (main administrator only).

- Each row is one program: code, description, department, number of years, and Active/Inactive.
- Change a row and press **Save** on that row.
- Add a program at the bottom using the empty row, then press **Add**. New programs appear in the roster filter immediately — no code changes needed.
- Set a program to **Inactive** to take it out of the roster's program filter. Students already in that program keep their records and history.

## 7. If something looks wrong

| What you see | What it means |
|---|---|
| *Could not load…* on an editor screen | The portal couldn't read the saved text. press that screen’s reload button (or leave the screen and come back); if it keeps happening, tell the administrator. |
| A page keeps its old wording after saving | That page has content saved in Website Pages, which wins over the heading on Page Banners. Edit it in Website Pages. |
| *That photo is too large* | The picture was still too big after shrinking. Save it smaller (or take a new photo at a lower quality) and try again. |
| A photo looks stretched or cut off | Photos are cropped to fill their space. Landscape pictures work best for banners and slide photos. |
| *The College workbook is not available* | The portal can't reach the College SIS file. Check that it's still shared with the school account, or ask the administrator to update the `COLLEGE_SS_ID` setting. |
| A student is missing from the roster | Check the filters first (**Clear** resets them), then look in the College SIS workbook. The roster only shows what's in that workbook's STUDENTS sheet. |
| A program isn't in the dropdown | It may be set to Inactive in Manage Programs. |

---

## Who can see what

- **College Roster, search and filters:** anyone who can sign in to the dashboard. It is view-only.
- **Homepage Hero, Homepage Sections, Page Banners, Website Pages, the About Moreh Academy editor and Manage Programs:** the main administrator only.

Every save is recorded in **System Administration → Activity Log**, so changes can always be traced.
